# AgriWise_v2
# Intelligent Agricultural Decision Platform 🌱

## Overview
Farmers today have access to a massive amount of agricultural information, but this data is highly fragmented. To make a successful harvest, a farmer must act as a weather analyst, agronomist, market researcher, and logistics planner. 

This project bridges that gap by connecting the entire agricultural decision lifecycle into one continuous, intelligent platform. We augment the farmer's intuition with data, shifting the process from isolated guesswork to an end-to-end decision chain.

## The Decision Chain
Our platform connects multiple data points to guide the farmer through a logical workflow:
**Farm Conditions → Crop → Seed → Inputs → Cost → Demand → Buyer → Transport → Estimated Profit**

## Key Features
* 🧠 **Explainable AI Recommendation Engine:** Evaluates climate, soil, water, and market parameters simultaneously to recommend highly suitable crops, showing exactly *why* a crop was recommended.
* 🌾 **Smart Seed & Input Ranking:** Ranks available seed varieties and required fertilizers based on the specific farm profile and crop requirements.
* 📊 **Market Intelligence:** Connects cultivation plans with real-world market demand, historical price trends, and regional supply shortages.
* 🚚 **Logistics & Buyer Discovery:** Helps farmers plan the post-harvest route by connecting them with potential buyers and transportation providers.
* 💰 **Profitability Engine:** Combines estimated cultivation costs, expected yield, and market prices to generate realistic estimated profitability scenarios.
* 📱 **Farmer-First UI/UX:** A modular, step-by-step interface designed for simplicity, using visual scores and clear recommendations instead of overwhelming dashboards.

## Architecture
The platform is built on a multi-layer architecture designed for geographic and functional scalability:
1. **Data Layer:** Ingests data from weather APIs, market databases, and soil parameters.
2. **Decision Engine:** Processes parameters to generate suitability scores.
3. **User Interface:** Separates the workflow into distinct, purpose-driven pages (Farm Analysis, Cultivation Plan, Market Intelligence, etc.).
